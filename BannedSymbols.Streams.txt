T:System.IO.FileStream;Durable file I/O goes through a SafeFileHandle + RandomAccess (write-at-offset + FlushToDisk) or a MemoryMappedFile, never a FileStream. A FileStream's managed write buffer and position cursor are the wrong model for a fsync-per-record append store: a buffered write that fails can linger and duplicate a record on retry. Open via File.OpenHandle and use RandomAccess. Corpus-reading test, benchmark, and workbench harnesses are exempt (see Directory.Build.props).
T:System.IO.BufferedStream;A BufferedStream reintroduces the managed buffer the durable storage layer deliberately avoids; open a SafeFileHandle and use RandomAccess instead.
